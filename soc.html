SOC / SIEM for Small & Medium Businesses

Delivering effective detection and response without the large team or budget assumptions.

Problem: Commercial SIEMs Are Expensive and Complex

Large SIEM solutions require integration experts, storage budgets, and tuning to avoid alert fatigue.

Impact

SMBs either under-provision (and miss incidents) or overspend on consultants and long-term logging costs.

Why Existing Solutions Fail

Many SIEMs assume continuous engineering resources, high data retention, and costly licensing that scale poorly for smaller orgs.

How Shadow-zet Solves It

Shadow-zet's SOC is embedded: curated detections, prioritized alerts, and AI-assisted triage tuned for smaller teams. Storage and retention policies are built-in and affordable.

Why an OS-Level Approach Is Necessary

Native ingestion reduces noise and ensures consistent schemas so automated triage and response are trustworthy and faster.

Benefits for SMBs

  • Faster response: prebuilt playbooks and contextualized alerts.
  • Lower cost: fewer third-party services and predictable licensing.
  • Smaller team friendly: AI assistance and guided runbooks for non-expert responders.
  • Compliance-ready: built-in audit logs and exportable reports.
