Investor Brief: Shadow-zet OS

A platform addressing a clear market gap: resilient, integrated OS-level security and operational capability for businesses and educational partners.

Problem & Market Opportunity

Tool sprawl, a global cybersecurity skills shortage, and rising costs create an addressable market across SMBs, enterprises, and education providers. Shadow-zet targets reductions in TCO and operational risk, unlocking predictable revenue from licensing, services, and certification programs.

Why Shadow-zet is Differentiated

  • OS-first approach: native telemetry and policy are harder for competitors to replicate.
  • Embedded labs & certification: creates recurring engagement and a recruiting moat.
  • SMB-focused SOC: product-market fit with predictable pricing and lower support needs.

Go-to-Market & Revenue Streams

  • Subscription licensing for core OS capabilities.
  • Certification and training subscriptions for education and enterprise customers.
  • Professional services for migration and customized deployment at scale.

Risk & Mitigation

Platform risk is real: adoption requires changes to operations. Shadow-zet mitigates this with phased deployment blueprints, hybrid support modes, and strong compliance/audit features to reduce legal friction.

Call to Action

We welcome investor conversations with a focus on accelerating product-market fit in SMBs, expanding certification partnerships, and scaling distributed deployments.
